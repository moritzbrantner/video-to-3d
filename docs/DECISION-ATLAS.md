# Decision Atlas

Product: `video-to-3d`. This is the repository's single source of truth for **what the product should be**, not a feature-progress checklist.

**Open question:** 8.5.1 — Post-v1 cloud policy.

## Reading and editing

- Each entry has an **explicit, stable ID**. Its position in the Markdown list never determines its identity; never renumber or reuse IDs. Headings are only navigation; historical Question IDs retain their original numbers even when filed with a different topic.
- **Everything written here is owner-approved product intent.** A `[decision]` is approved as written; uncertainty is written as a `[question]`. An answered Question stays visible with its ID and points to the answering Decision. `[x]` / `[ ]` appear only as built / not-built evidence children under a Decision (coding-tooling Atlas 1.1.4); they never mark approval, and a Decision without them is simply not audited.
- Entries are the numbered list under **Entries**: topic `N`, area `N.M`, entry `N.M.K`. Entries from the earlier two-level layout (for example `1.4`) are now `N.M.1` (`3.1` is `3.1.3`) and keep their old ID as the legacy alias `video-to-3d/<old ID>`; prose cross-references such as "See 1.4" use those legacy IDs.
- Decisions and Questions are the only semantic leaf kinds. `[question]` preserves Question identity even after being answered. Do not introduce additional kinds for constraints, deferral, proposals or implementation.
- Keep Questions next to the topic they concern rather than in a separate Questions category. Follow current Decisions; ask one relevant unanswered Question at a time when it materially affects work. A Question may remain deferred without blocking unrelated implementation.
- New answers append a Decision with `Answers ID`. To revise a Decision, strike the outdated wording and write its replacement after it, or record an explicitly linked `Supersedes ID` Decision; IDs stay stable. Do not silently rewrite settled product direction.
- Implementation and verification evidence is separate from owner approval. A Decision is not a claim that code or acceptance tests already satisfy it.
- **Product-feature focus:** record only consequential choices about user workflows, supported inputs/outputs, scene behavior, capabilities, and deliberate tradeoffs. Ordinary defects, performance regressions, implementation techniques, and generic testing requirements belong in GitHub issues, `AGENTS.md`, and acceptance specifications—not as newly invented product Decisions. Historical entries retain their IDs until separately reviewed rather than silently deleted.
- Preserve the current owner-approved meaning when reformatting; Git history and reviewed issue/PR discussions provide provenance.

## Entries

- **1** Product behaviour and scene representation
  - **1.1** Major scene decomposition is the default.
    - **1.1.1** [decision] Individual walls, doors, windows, furniture, and other meaningful objects are independently editable scene identities by default. Disconnected triangles of one mesh do not count as separate objects. Finer/coarser granularity may become configurable later. See 3.2 and 7.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.1`.
  - **1.2** Preserve observation-only and AI-completed scene modes.
    - **1.2.1** [decision] The product exposes both geometry supported by observations and an AI-completed scene with plausible missing geometry. Completion must not be presented as filmed evidence. See 2.3, 3.4, and 5.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.2`.
  - **1.3** Editable meshes are the default DCC representation.
    - **1.3.1** [decision] Blender receives editable meshes by default. Higher-fidelity representations such as Gaussian splats may accompany them. A representation switch is required whenever both mesh and splat representations actually exist; splats do not substitute for mesh editability. See 3.3 and 4.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.3`.
  - **1.4** Ordinary handheld video is the product target.
    - **1.4.1** [decision] Imperfect handheld recordings are supported product input. A guided capture may be used as an engineering milestone, but cannot become the final product limitation. See 3.1. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.4`.
  - **1.5** Generated content requires video-grounded validation.
    - **1.5.1** [decision] Plausible objects or missing geometry may be generated when needed, but placement and appearance must be checked against retained video evidence. Unsupported predictions remain uncertain rather than becoming verified geometry. See 2.3, 3.4, and 5.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.5`.
  - **1.6** Normal processing is automatic.
    - **1.6.1** [decision] Normal runs require no human or agent orchestration. Optional review checkpoints may appear before expensive local stages so a user can correct proposed object identities or scene decisions. See 4.4. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.6`.
  - **1.7** Hybrid per-region mesh/splat viewing is the default when both exist.
    - **1.7.1** [decision] Answers 8.4.1 (owner selected B). Show high-fidelity splat surroundings and independently editable mesh objects together in one aligned scene when those representations exist. Let users select individual objects or scene regions and switch between mesh and splat display wherever both representations are actually available, without replacing the rest of the scene, jumping the camera, or modifying the underlying geometry, transforms, object identity, or evidence provenance. Meshes remain the editable representation and the default Blender/DCC export; splats are visual companions, not a substitute for mesh editability. When a region has only one representation, show it honestly and indicate that the other is unavailable rather than fabricating it. Preserve observation-only versus AI-completed provenance independently of display choice. Do not make optional splat generation or optimization a prerequisite for the initial three-real-video editable-mesh acceptance gate. See 1.2–1.3, 2.3, 3.2–3.4, and 4.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/1.7`.

  - **1.8** Show validated reconstruction progressively.
    - **1.8.1** [decision] Owner answer (2026-10-17, steering Q9 A). During automatic reconstruction, show accepted cameras, environment geometry and editable objects as their validated outputs become available. Keep scene identities, coordinate alignment, provenance and observation-only versus completed distinctions stable between updates; identify unfinished, weak, unsupported and unavailable regions rather than implying that a partial preview is complete. An intermediate preview does not satisfy the three-video Blender product gate (3.6). Reuse the shared viewer and resumable project runner. See #71 and #77.

- **2** Reconstruction architecture and evidence
  - **2.1** Rust owns reusable reconstruction semantics.
    - **2.1.1** [decision] Feature detection, matching, camera-path estimation, point generation, evidence validation/fusion, and shared scene data contracts belong in `crates/video-to-3d-core`. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.1`.
  - **2.2** Reconstruction backends are evidence providers, not geometry authorities.
    - **2.2.1** [decision] Classical, learned multi-view, and generative backends must cross the versioned `ReconstructionEvidenceView` boundary before their output can participate in shared validation or fusion. See 2.1 and 2.3. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.2`.
  - **2.3** Evidence provenance remains explicit.
    - **2.3.1** [decision] Geometric multi-view evidence, learned evidence, image-revalidated completion, and generative completion remain distinguishable. Generative or completed geometry must never be silently relabeled as observed geometry. See 1.2 and 1.5. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.3`.
  - **2.4** Avoid unnecessary geometry copies.
    - **2.4.1** [decision] Prefer borrowed/ranged evidence and explicit provenance metadata. Materialize owned snapshots only when export or external interchange requires ownership. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.4`.
  - **2.5** WASM is an adapter boundary.
    - **2.5.1** [decision] `crates/video-to-3d-wasm` adapts shared logic; it does not duplicate reconstruction semantics from 2.1. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.5`.
  - **2.6** Native providers must reuse shared validation/fusion semantics.
    - **2.6.1** [decision] Tauri/native workers may execute heavyweight local providers, but their evidence enters through the same authority boundary from 2.2 rather than creating a second native reconstruction authority. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/2.6`.

  - **2.7** COLMAP is the provisional native reconstruction reference and preferred provider.
    - **2.7.1** [decision] Owner answers (2026-10-17, steering Q2 A, Q3 native-COLMAP-first, Q4 A and Q7 C). Prefer local COLMAP for desktop/native reconstruction, with the owned Rust pipeline as fallback and the continuing browser/WASM baseline. First integrate COLMAP sparse camera/point reconstruction using the existing pinned golden/reference work; compare its dense reconstruction on the same evidence before using dense output where it materially improves accepted scene quality. COLMAP is an evidence provider, not a second geometry authority: all accepted output crosses the versioned Rust validation/fusion boundary from 2.2. Keep COLMAP as the independent quality oracle while Rust catches up. Automatically make Rust the preferred native provider when independent comparison on the three pinned real-video fixtures demonstrates comparable or better accepted camera/scene coverage and independently editable objects, without unacceptable runtime or memory regressions; no additional owner approval is required for that evidence-based promotion. Fixture-specific criteria remain owned by 3.1–3.6 and 7.3, not guessed universal thresholds. See #204, #232, #81.
  - **2.8** Escalate to learned geometry only where reconstruction remains weak.
    - **2.8.1** [decision] Owner answer (2026-10-17, steering Q6 A). After preferred classical reconstruction, invoke the approved local MapAnything candidate only for weak or missing evidence, under measured hardware limits and explicit provider availability. Retain strong accepted geometry; learned camera/depth/geometry proposals must cross Rust-owned evidence validation and retain their learned provenance. Do not run every provider unconditionally or treat missing learned inference as observed geometry. See 2.2–2.3, 4.5–4.7, #79 and #81.

    - **3.2.1** [decision] Meaningful architectural elements and objects remain separately identifiable, selectable, and transformable through the scene pipeline.     - **3.6.1** [decision] The aggregate gate is green only when the complete local video → scene → Blender workflow passes for all three pinned real clips. - **4** Local platform and execution
  - **4.1** Initial release processing is fully local.
    - **4.1.1** [decision] No cloud inference, provider calls, source uploads, or derived-frame uploads are permitted in the initial release. Cloud execution requires a later explicit owner decision, subject to the persistent source-video privacy invariant in 4.3. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.1`.
  - **4.2** Browser and desktop share one application interface.
    - **4.2.1** [decision] GitHub Pages/browser provides lightweight reconstruction and inspectable previews. Desktop/local-native execution provides heavyweight AI and Blender export. The user-facing product remains one interface rather than two separate applications. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.2`.
  - **4.3** Browser-side code owns video decoding and frame sampling.
    - **4.3.1** [decision] This remains true inside the Tauri desktop shell. Native workers must not create a competing authoritative decode/sampling path. Uploaded source video must not leave the device; this repository privacy invariant also constrains any later cloud policy. See 4.1 and 4.2. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.3`.
  - **4.4** Optional review checkpoints occur before expensive local stages.
    - **4.4.1** [decision] The automatic default from 1.6 remains intact. Review checkpoints may allow corrections to object identity, uncertainty, or scene placement while preserving original evidence and deterministic invalidation/resume behavior. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.4`.
  - **4.5** Hardware selection adapts locally and reports limits honestly.
    - **4.5.1** [decision] Provider/model choice adapts to available CPU/GPU resources. CPU-only operation must remain usable, potentially at reduced quality or speed, and requires a CPU-only smoke check. The approved CPU-only baseline is in Decision 4.6 (Answers 8.2.1). Requested versus attainable quality and unavailable capabilities must be explicit; there is no hidden cloud fallback. See 4.1. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.5`.
  - **4.6** CPU-only operation preserves a useful observation-only workflow.
    - **4.6.1** [decision] Answers 8.2.1 (owner selected B). On machines without a suitable GPU, users can locally reconstruct video-supported geometry, inspect the accepted scene, and export supported independently editable geometry via the existing scene/Blender handoff. CPU-only processing may be slower or lower quality; GPU-dependent AI-completion providers and their representations may be unavailable. Report unavailable or degraded capabilities and reasons explicitly rather than silently skipping stages, fabricating observed geometry, calling a cloud service, or marking a missing AI-completed representation as present. A CPU-only smoke test must exercise real local observed reconstruction, inspection and supported export when accepted geometry is available, and honest unavailable-completion reporting when it is not. This does not waive the full three-real-video product gate on declared capable local hardware in 3.6. See 1.2–1.5, 3.3–3.6, and 4.1–4.5. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.6`.
  - **4.7** First learned multi-view provider prioritizes a reproducible 8 GB local GPU fit.
    - **4.7.1** [decision] Answers 8.1.1 (owner delegated provider choice based on GeForce RTX 3060 with 8 GB VRAM). Evaluate `facebook/map-anything-apache` (Apache 2.0 checkpoint) first as the candidate local learned multi-view evidence provider, using its documented memory-efficient inference, mixed precision and minimal inference minibatch, and existing camera/calibration inputs when available. This is a prioritized **candidate, not a verified claim that it fits 8 GB or satisfies the product**. Before enabling it as a supported production profile, run a reproducible native on-device benchmark on an RTX 3060-class **8 GB** GPU, recording model/checkpoint and license, software versions, image resolution, view counts, actual peak device VRAM, runtime, success/OOM and accepted geometric evidence on relevant pinned clips. Select the largest demonstrably supported batch/view policy rather than guessing; if it cannot fit or produce useful accepted evidence, report unavailable capability, retain the existing local observed-geometry fallback, and revisit provider selection using measured evidence instead of silently substituting a model. Learned output remains subject to the common Rust `ReconstructionEvidenceView` validation/fusion boundary, explicit provenance and the unchanged three-real-video scene-to-Blender product acceptance. No cloud inference, media upload or implicit CPU/GPU equivalence. Source: MapAnything repository (`https://github.com/facebookresearch/map-anything`) and Apache checkpoint (`https://huggingface.co/facebook/map-anything-apache`). See 2.2–2.3, 3.1–3.6, 4.1–4.6. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/4.7`.

  - **4.8** Acquire the preferred native COLMAP runtime on first need.
    - **4.8.1** [decision] Owner answer (2026-10-17, steering Q5 A). On first desktop/native use, automatically acquire a pinned compatible local COLMAP runtime when it is not already cached; reuse cached installations idempotently and support offline processing once acquired. Acquisition must not upload video or derived media. If the runtime cannot be acquired or run, report the limitation and use the Rust fallback without cloud processing. This is not a mandate to bundle COLMAP in every desktop package or require manual setup. See #204, 4.1–4.3.
  - **4.9** Standard is the default reconstruction quality.
    - **4.9.1** [decision] Owner answer (2026-10-17, steering Q8 A). Default new user-facing scene projects to Standard quality: automatically produce useful environment reconstruction, independently editable objects and selectively escalated local completion where evidence warrants it. Preview and Production remain explicit options. Keep actual supported quality and unavailable stages visible rather than silently downgrading the selected mode. See 1.1–1.6, 4.5 and #71.
  - **4.10** Closing desktop processing pauses and reopening resumes.
    - **4.10.1** [decision] Owner answer (2026-10-17, steering Q12 A). Closing the desktop application safely stops in-progress native reconstruction instead of continuing under a background service. Retain verified project receipts/artifacts; on reopening the project, automatically resume unfinished local work from the last valid stage, without rerunning unchanged expensive operations. Surface unavailable/failed steps honestly. Browser-only execution retains ordinary browser lifecycle limits. See #235, 1.6 and 5.3.

- **5** Evidence quality, placement and corrections
  - **5.1** Every algorithmic change requires deterministic Rust coverage and observable diagnostics.
    - **5.1.1** [decision] Algorithmic changes must have deterministic Rust tests and a diagnostic signal that makes failure observable in the UI. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/5.1`.
  - **5.2** Object placement is accepted only against retained source evidence.
    - **5.2.1** [decision] Scale, pose, support relationships, occlusion, depth ordering, and appearance should be validated by reprojecting candidates into registered source views. Contradictory views prevent silent acceptance. See 1.5 and 3.4. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/5.2`.
  - **5.3** Corrections must not rewrite source evidence.
    - **5.3.1** [decision] User corrections alter proposed interpretation or generated/completed scene state, not the original captured evidence. Derived artifacts should invalidate only along their actual dependency graph. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/5.3`.
  - **5.4** Provide meaningful corrections throughout the processing pipeline, starting in source frames.
    - **5.4.1** [decision] Answers 8.3.1 (owner chose B as the first slice). Every meaningful processing stage should ultimately allow the user to review and correct its derived interpretations or outputs when appropriate, rather than forcing an all-or-nothing rerun. Begin with optional video-based correction of object identities, masks and cross-frame tracks in sampled source frames before expensive local reconstruction or completion stages. Preserve automatic processing as the default; no user checkpoint is mandatory for normal operation. Corrections must not modify original video or reclassify generated guesses as observed evidence, must retain provenance, and should invalidate/recompute only dependent downstream work through the existing deterministic project runner. Other stage-specific correction affordances may be delivered incrementally as their outputs become inspectable; this direction does not require inventing an editor for every internal algorithmic substep in the first bounded slice. See 1.6, 4.4, 5.2 and 5.3. Legacy alias: `video-to-3d/5.4`.
